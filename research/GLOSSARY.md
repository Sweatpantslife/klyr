# Glossary: ADHD and Corpus Vocabulary

Fast decoding for every term the corpus leans on. Each entry carries a **status tag**, because in ADHD vocabulary the difference between a diagnosis, a research construct, and a community coinage is load-bearing — a term can encode real UX truth while having thin science, and the corpus uses plenty of both. Definitions are deliberately short; evidence, numbers, and nuance live in the linked docs. If a doc introduces a new term, it must be added here with a tag (see [CONVENTIONS](_meta/CONVENTIONS.md)).

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [clinical] | Established clinical or diagnostic concept (DSM/ICD, major-org guidance, named clinical principle) |
| [research] | Research construct with a peer-reviewed literature |
| [community] | Lived-experience term from the ADHD community; encodes real UX truth, usually little or no direct research |
| [contested] | Popular and useful, but scientifically unsettled or unvalidated |
| [design] | Product, methodology, or design vocabulary — including terms this corpus or Klyr coined (flagged in the entry) |

## A

- **ADHD burnout** [community] — A cycle of overcommitting, masking, and crashing into exhaustion that ADHD adults widely report; not a validated clinical construct. The nearest peer-reviewed anchor is autistic burnout (chronic exhaustion, loss of skills, reduced stimulus tolerance, typically 3+ months). More: [when-to-back-off](product/when-to-back-off.md), [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md).
- **ADHD paralysis** [community] — Being unable to begin (task paralysis), unable to pick (choice paralysis), or unable to think (overwhelm freeze) despite genuinely wanting to act. Clinically plausible as the simultaneous failure of executive, emotional, and reward systems at the start line. More: [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md).
- **ADHD presentations** [clinical] — DSM-5-TR describes ADHD (a neurodevelopmental condition of self-regulation) via three presentations: predominantly inattentive, predominantly hyperactive-impulsive, and combined. Deliberately renamed from "subtypes" because they are snapshots people move between, typically drifting toward inattentive with age. More: [adhd-overview](foundations/adhd-overview.md).
- **ADHD tax** [community] — The recurring financial and emotional cost of symptoms: late fees, expired groceries, impulse buys, missed payments, forgotten subscriptions, plus the self-blame that follows. Increasingly quantified (one UK survey: ~£1,600/year in extra costs). More: [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md), [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md).
- **Alert fatigue (habituation)** [research] — The reliable decay of response to repeated, identical notifications; clinicians override 49–96% of clinical alerts, and task-app reminders die the same way. The fix is fewer, better-timed, form-varying prompts — never more volume. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md), [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md).
- **Attention residue** [research] — Sophie Leroy's finding that an unfinished prior task keeps consuming cognitive capacity after you switch to the next one. See *ready-to-resume note* for the evidenced countermeasure. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md).
- **AuDHD** [community] — Co-occurring autism and ADHD (roughly 38–40% of autistic people also have ADHD). The corpus's sharpest internal design tension: ADHD-optimized novelty, surprise, and stimulation are exactly what can hurt the autistic side. More: [populations-and-variation](foundations/populations-and-variation.md).

## B

- **Behavioral activation** [clinical] — Evidence-based depression treatment built on graded re-engagement; its core lesson is that action precedes motivation, not the reverse. Grounds both Klyr's start-tiny mechanics and the rule that backing off means shrinking demands, not removing all structure. More: [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md), [when-to-back-off](product/when-to-back-off.md).
- **Body doubling** [community] — Working alongside another person, in the room or on video, whose mere presence makes starting and continuing easier. Community-discovered and research-emerging: 85% of 220 neurodivergent respondents in the first academic survey reported it helps; no RCTs yet. More: [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md), [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md).
- **Boom-bust cycle** [community] — Overcommitting during high-capacity stretches (often while masking and hyperfocusing), then crashing. It guarantees every user will eventually disappear from any system calibrated to their best week. More: [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md), [when-to-back-off](product/when-to-back-off.md).
- **Bullet journaling (BuJo)** [design] — Ryder Carroll's analog method (rapid logging, migration, self-made spreads), invented because of his ADHD. Its lessons for Klyr: a blank page absorbs absence without judgment, migration doubles as a natural review, and restyling grants sanctioned novelty; craft perfectionism is its trap. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).

## C

- **Capture** [design] — Getting a thought out of the head and into the system before it evaporates, which happens in seconds. Corpus bar: sub-3-second, zero-decision, available from anywhere; spending friction here is never legitimate. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md), [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md).
- **Chronotype** [research] — A person's biological timing preference for sleep and alertness. ADHD skews strongly late — "late chronotypes living in a morning-shaped world" — so morning-anchored defaults are systematically mistimed for the core user. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md).
- **COGA** [design] — W3C's Cognitive and Learning Disabilities Accessibility Task Force and its "Making Content Usable" note (2021): the closest thing to an official cognitive-accessibility standard. Its eight objectives are Klyr's design floor and release checklist. More: [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md).
- **Cognitive load** [research] — The working-memory cost of using an interface; extraneous (design-caused) load competes directly with the user's actual task, and ADHD pays a premium. Klyr absorbs complexity instead of exporting it (Tesler's law) and treats choice overload as a documented abandonment risk. More: [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md).

## D

- **Dark patterns** [design] — Interface designs that manipulate users against their own interests: fake urgency, guilt copy, streak hostage-taking, hard-to-cancel subscriptions, silent trial conversions. Several exploit ADHD impulsivity and shame specifically; Klyr bans them outright. More: [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md), [anti-patterns](product/anti-patterns.md).
- **Default mode network (DMN)** [research] — The brain network active during rest and self-referential thought. In ADHD it is insufficiently suppressed during demanding tasks — the leading account of excessive spontaneous mind-wandering. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md), [adhd-overview](foundations/adhd-overview.md).
- **Delay discounting** [research] — How steeply a reward loses subjective value as it moves into the future; also called temporal discounting, hyperbolic in shape. Steeper in ADHD (meta-analytic d = 0.43) and the mechanism behind deadline panic, abandoned long-term projects, and the pull of the immediate. More: [dopamine-and-motivation](foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md), [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md).
- **Delayed sleep phase** [clinical] — Circadian timing shifted late: in ADHD, melatonin onset is delayed roughly 45 minutes in children and 90 in adults, sleep-wake timing is delayed in 73–78%, and diagnosable delayed sleep phase disorder is far more common than in the general population. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md), [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md).
- **Demand sensitivity** [community] — The reported flip of a want into an aversive chore the moment it becomes an obligation, even a self-imposed one ("the fastest way to stop wanting to do something is to put it on a to-do list"). An organizer app is structurally a demand machine, so Klyr must actively defuse this. More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).
- **Distraction-by-anticipation** [design] — Corpus working term for the intrusive idea that must be acted on *now* because waiting means losing it — a memory-driven distraction, not an impulse-control failure. The single strongest argument for a two-keystroke capture inbox. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md).
- **Doom piles** [community] — Piles, physical or digital, made by relocating clutter without organizing it — backronymed "Didn't Organize, Only Moved." Under cue-dependent attention they are rational external memory, not laziness. More: [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md), [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md).
- **Doomscrolling** [community] — Compulsive continued feed-scrolling. Relevant because feeds' engineered mechanics (infinite scroll, variable rewards) capture exactly the bored, emotion-regulating states ADHD users are in while avoiding tasks — mechanics Klyr is banned from borrowing. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md), [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md).
- **Doorway effect** [research] — Walking through a doorway (an "event boundary") measurably purges working memory. The everyday mechanism behind "why did I come into this room?" and a reason context changes silently kill intentions. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md).
- **Dopamine menu** [community] — A pre-made menu of quick, genuinely enjoyable activities to reach for when the brain demands stimulation, so the choice does not default to the infinite feed. Community-grade strategy: coherent mechanism, no trials. More: [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md).

## E

- **Eisenhower matrix** [design] — The urgent/important 2×2 prioritization grid. It asks users to perform exactly the discrimination ADHD impairs (felt urgency vs. real importance), so it works as an assisted lens, not a daily self-serve tool. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **Elastic recurrence** [design] — Klyr mechanic: recurring tasks are cycles, not clones — a missed cycle compresses into one current "next instance" (never "overdue ×14"), cadence stretches with reality, and declared low-capacity periods skip rather than stack. More: [feature-directions](product/feature-directions.md), [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md).
- **Emotional dysregulation** [research] — Emotions arrive at normal-to-full intensity but the executive brake that moderates them is weak; Barkley's term is deficient emotional self-regulation (DESR). Treated by leading researchers as a core feature of adult ADHD (est. 34–70% affected) despite being absent from DSM criteria. More: [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md).
- **Executive function (EF)** [research] — The self-directed mental actions (inhibition, working memory, planning, emotional control, and so on) that steer behavior toward goals over time. ADHD shows real but moderate, non-universal EF weaknesses, and EF output fluctuates hour to hour — capacity is a variable state, not a fixed trait. More: [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md).
- **Externalization** [clinical] — Barkley's core compensation principle: internal information is weak stimulus control in ADHD, so time, information, and motivation must be made physical and present in the environment. The theoretical license for Klyr's existence. More: [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md).

## F

- **Flow** [research] — Absorbed, seemingly effortless engagement in an optimally challenging activity, with goal-directedness intact. Whether ADHD hyperfocus is simply flow by another name is genuinely contested; the corpus treats them as overlapping but not identical. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md).
- **Friction asymmetry** [design] — Corpus rule for where interaction cost belongs: capture, completion, and coming back must cost nearly nothing, while friction is legitimately spent only on destruction and self-identified distractions. More: [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md).

## G

- **Gamification** [research] — Game elements (points, levels, streaks, creatures) applied to non-game tasks. Meta-analyses show real but moderate, heterogeneous benefits — strongest on engagement — with a reliable novelty dip around week 4; the honest claim is "sustains engagement," never "treats ADHD." More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).
- **Graceful degradation** [design] — Designing routines and plans to shrink rather than shatter under low capacity: menus not scripts, a minimum-viable version of every routine, metrics that forgive. More: [habits-and-routines](daily-life/habits-and-routines.md), [when-to-back-off](product/when-to-back-off.md).
- **GTD (Getting Things Done)** [design] — David Allen's methodology: capture everything, clarify next actions, park them in a trusted system, review weekly. Its capture/next-action/trust core is gold for ADHD (it *is* externalization); its weekly review is the single point of failure. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).

## H

- **Habit loop (cue–routine–reward)** [research] — A habit is a learned context-cue → behavior association, strengthened by reward until the cue alone triggers the behavior (automaticity). Median time to automaticity is ~66 days with enormous individual range (18–254+ days); the "21-day rule" is a myth. More: [habits-and-routines](daily-life/habits-and-routines.md).
- **Habit stacking** [community] — Attaching a new tiny behavior to an existing automatic one ("after I pour coffee, I take my meds"); from Fogg's anchoring and Clear's popularization. Partly transfers to ADHD, but quietly assumes the anchor is automatic and the stack is remembered — both shaky. More: [habits-and-routines](daily-life/habits-and-routines.md).
- **Hyperfocus** [contested] — Prolonged, intense absorption marked by timelessness, ignored bodily needs, and difficulty stopping or switching. Real to users and now measurable (Adult Hyperfocus Questionnaire), but thin in the literature, absent from the DSM, and positively correlated with distractibility — the same weak attentional control produces both. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md).

## I

- **Implementation intentions** [research] — Gollwitzer's if-then planning ("If it's 9am Monday, then I open the report"), which delegates initiation to a pre-decided cue instead of in-the-moment willpower. The best-evidenced portable technique in the corpus (meta-analytic d ≈ 0.65; replicated in ADHD samples). More: [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md), [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md).
- **Incentive salience ("wanting")** [research] — Berridge's term for the dopamine-dependent pull toward a reward cue, distinct from "liking" (hedonic pleasure, which is not dopamine-dependent). Why making a task pleasant does not make an ADHD user want to start it. More: [dopamine-and-motivation](foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md).
- **Interest-based nervous system** [contested] — Dodson's clinical heuristic that ADHD engagement runs on interest, novelty, challenge/competition, urgency, and passion rather than importance. No peer-reviewed operationalization, but directionally consistent with the reward literature and with how users describe themselves — Klyr speaks this language without building falsifiable claims on it. More: [dopamine-and-motivation](foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md).

## K

- **Kanban** [design] — Visualizing work as cards moving through columns (To do / Doing / Done). The corpus's strongest structural fit for ADHD: visible state offloads working memory and the Done column is evidence against "I did nothing" — but the backlog column becomes a shame landfill. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).

## M

- **Maintenance ritual** [design] — Corpus term for the recurring upkeep every productivity system demands (weekly review, migration, filing, board grooming): boring, delayed-reward, EF-heavy — the exact task profile ADHD handles worst, and the spot where every methodology dies. Klyr's rule: automate the ritual. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **Masking** [research] — Effortfully suppressing or compensating for ADHD traits to appear fine. A leading explanation for late and missed diagnosis (especially in women) and a driver of overcommitment, exhaustion, and boom-bust cycles. More: [adhd-overview](foundations/adhd-overview.md), [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md).
- **Mind-wandering (spontaneous)** [research] — Unintentional, unbidden thought drift, tied to DMN regulation failure. Correlates with ADHD inattention at r = .77 and with impairment at r = .81 — internal distraction usually outweighs external. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md).
- **MIT (Most Important Tasks)** [design] — Daily-plan method of picking 1–3 tasks that count as the day won (cousins: Ivy Lee, 1-3-5). Tiny plans fit ADHD capacity; picking the MIT is the hard part, and strict ordering breaks on energy variance. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **Monotropism** [research] — Murray, Lawson & Lesser's model of autistic attention as a narrow, deep tunnel. Explains why many AuDHD users need change announced rather than sprung, and why "surprise me" is a threat, not a treat, for a large minority. More: [populations-and-variation](foundations/populations-and-variation.md).

## N

- **Next action** [design] — GTD's unit of doability: the specific physical next step ("email Sam the draft," not "taxes"). Items without one get skipped on every scan — and since decomposition is itself an impaired executive step in ADHD, Klyr must help produce next actions, not demand them. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md), [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md).
- **Novelty honeymoon** [design] — Corpus term for the roughly two weeks a new system runs on novelty dopamine before habituation sets in — "every productivity app works for two weeks." Klyr's single biggest product risk; design for month three. More: [dopamine-and-motivation](foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md), [app-landscape](product/app-landscape.md).
- **Novelty rotation** [design] — Deliberately scheduled variety (themes, modes, celebration content, mechanics) that channels ADHD novelty-seeking inside the product instead of losing the user to a fresh app. Community-grade strategy, planned into Klyr's roadmap so declining engagement routes to variety, not shame. More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md), [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md).
- **Now/not-now** [community] — Clinician-popularized shorthand for ADHD's binary time horizon: events are either immediate or unreal, so something two hours out and something two weeks out can feel equally distant. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md).

## O

- **Object permanence (colloquial)** [community] — Community shorthand for "out of sight, out of mind": invisible things stop existing behaviorally. Not literal Piagetian object permanence — the real mechanism is cue-dependent memory and attention — but it encodes real UX truth: piles are rational external memory, and putting things away feels like deleting them. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md).
- **Overdue amnesty** [design] — Klyr core mechanic: tasks that age past their moment quietly de-emphasize and move to a calm "parked" state with a no-judgment resurface prompt ("Still matter? Shrink it? Let it go?"), making a 47-item red overdue list structurally impossible. More: [feature-directions](product/feature-directions.md), [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md).
- **Overjustification effect** [research] — Expected tangible rewards eroding intrinsic interest in an already-enjoyable activity. Klyr's rule: reward the boring, never the loved; prefer informational feedback over controlling incentives. More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).

## P

- **PARA** [design] — Tiago Forte's Projects/Areas/Resources/Archives taxonomy, organized by actionability. The tiny taxonomy is smart; per-capture filing decisions are a maintenance ritual that quietly dies — capture-first with search-over-sort fits ADHD better. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **PDA (pathological demand avoidance)** [contested] — A profile, described mostly within autism, of extreme anxiety-driven avoidance of demands — including wanted and self-imposed ones. Clinically contested as a construct; the everyday demand-sensitivity phenomenon it names is real and common in ADHD. More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).
- **Planning fallacy** [research] — The systematic human tendency to underestimate how long tasks will take; it compounds with ADHD time blindness into visibly failed days. Klyr corrects it *for* the user: learn real durations from actuals, pad by default, surface plan-vs-hours collisions at plan time. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md), [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **Point of performance** [clinical] — Barkley: the place and time in someone's natural setting where they fail to use what they know. Help works there — the right cue at the right moment — and mostly does not transfer from planning sessions, tutorials, or Sunday reviews. More: [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md), [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md).
- **Pomodoro** [design] — Timer-based work in short intervals (classically 25 minutes) with breaks. Rests on clinical consensus and massive community endorsement rather than RCTs; the ADHD adaptation that matters is flexible intervals, easy on-ramps, and never yanking the user out of flow. More: [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md).
- **Positive illusory bias** [research] — The documented ADHD tendency to over-rate one's own competence and improvement. It makes global self-ratings ("how productive were you this week?") unreliable as outcome measures — prefer validated scales anchored to concrete behaviors. More: [outcomes-and-measurement](product/outcomes-and-measurement.md).
- **Procrastination (mood-repair model)** [research] — Sirois & Pychyl's account: delay is short-term emotion regulation — escaping the bad feeling a task raises now and billing the future self. ADHD multiplies it (~75% of adults with ADHD classify as chronic procrastinators vs. ~35% of controls). More: [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md).
- **Productivity system graveyard** [community] — The trail of abandoned planners, apps, and notebooks produced by the system-collapse–shame–new-system cycle: novelty at setup → maintenance ritual lapses → stale accusing debris → shame → avoidance → a new system with a clean slate. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md), [app-landscape](product/app-landscape.md).
- **Progressive disclosure** [design] — Showing core functionality first and revealing depth on demand, so power never costs first-day overwhelm. For ADHD it must pair with guaranteed resurfacing, because hidden can mean gone. More: [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md).
- **Prospective memory** [research] — "Remembering to remember": executing an intention later. Time-based intentions ("at 5pm") are the most reliably impaired in ADHD; event-based ones fare better when the cue is salient. Note that "remember to check the app" is itself a prospective-memory task — so the system must push. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md).
- **Prosthetic environment** [clinical] — Barkley's frame for lasting support: external scaffolds (cues, timers, visible state) that substitute for unreliable internal executive functions — and keep working only while they stay in place. Klyr is a prosthesis the user keeps, not a program they graduate from. More: [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md).

## R

- **Ready-to-resume note** [research] — Leroy's ~1-minute practice of noting, before switching away, where you stopped, what remains, and what to postpone; it measurably reduced attention residue across four studies. Basis for Klyr's bookmark-on-interrupt directions. More: [attention-and-hyperfocus](foundations/attention-and-hyperfocus.md), [feature-directions](product/feature-directions.md).
- **Rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD)** [contested] — Dodson's term for extreme, sometimes physically painful reactions to perceived rejection or criticism. Enormous community resonance but not a validated diagnosis, and several of Dodson's strongest claims are unsupported — yet the behavior it names (avoided inboxes, dreaded feedback, preemptive quitting) is product-decisive. More: [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md).
- **Restart-first design** [design] — Corpus inversion of tracker logic: since the load-bearing ADHD skill is resuming after a miss, the restart — not the streak — is the most polished path in the app: zero make-up work, no gap recap, celebrated resumes. More: [habits-and-routines](daily-life/habits-and-routines.md), [design-principles](product/design-principles.md).
- **Return-after-lapse** [design] — Klyr's North Star metric: of users who go quiet, how many come back and do something meaningful without being guilt-tripped into it. Chosen because abandonment is the market's defining fact and engagement metrics are novelty-confounded. More: [outcomes-and-measurement](product/outcomes-and-measurement.md), [app-landscape](product/app-landscape.md).
- **Revenge bedtime procrastination** [community] — Staying up late to reclaim the autonomy and stimulation the day never provided, converting today's unmet needs into tomorrow's morning debt. More: [daily-life-impact](daily-life/daily-life-impact.md).
- **Reward prediction error (RPE)** [research] — What dopamine neurons signal: the gap between expected and actual outcome, with responses migrating to the earliest reliable predictive cue. The most replicated ADHD reward finding is blunted striatal response during *anticipation* — plans don't pull; ticking the box does. More: [dopamine-and-motivation](foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md).

## S

- **Self-compassion** [research] — Neff's construct: self-kindness, common humanity, and mindfulness in the face of one's own failures. Adults with ADHD score lower on it, and it partially mediates the link between ADHD and poor mental health — the corpus's best-evidenced antidote to shame and a directly designable lever for tone. More: [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md).
- **Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** [research] — Motivation framework built on three needs: autonomy, competence, and relatedness. The safest foundation for Klyr's engagement layer, since reward-only designs risk "controlled motivation" that collapses when the rewards stop. More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).
- **Self-regulation** [research] — Managing one's own attention, emotion, motivation, and action toward goals over time. In Barkley's model executive function *is* self-regulation, making ADHD as much a self-regulation deficit as an attention deficit. More: [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md), [adhd-overview](foundations/adhd-overview.md).
- **Snooze-death** [design] — Corpus term for the reminder failure mode where snoozed means lost: each deferral weakens the cue until the item silently drops out of attention. Klyr's notification stack must answer it explicitly (escalation ladders, form changes, resurfacing). More: [ux-design-for-adhd](product/ux-design-for-adhd.md), [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md).
- **Spoon theory (spoons)** [community] — Christine Miserandino's chronic-illness metaphor, widely adopted by ADHDers: daily capacity is a limited, variable budget of "spoons," and the same task costs different amounts on different days. Basis for energy-based planning. More: [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md).
- **Streak** [design] — Consecutive-day counters. A potent loss-aversion motivator that turns hostage-taker: one miss resets to zero, fires the shame spiral, and users abandon the whole practice — so Klyr allows streak-like mechanics only with structural forgiveness (auto-applied freezes, decay-not-reset, cumulative counts, celebrated restarts). More: [habits-and-routines](daily-life/habits-and-routines.md), [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).

## T

- **Temporal Motivation Theory (TMT)** [research] — Motivation = (Expectancy × Value) / (1 + Impulsiveness × Delay). Compactly explains ADHD task behavior and hands Klyr four levers: raise expectancy, raise or reveal value, shorten delay, and stop fighting impulsiveness. More: [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).
- **Temporal myopia** [research] — Barkley's term for ADHD's nearsightedness in time: behavior is governed by the temporally near, so distant deadlines exert almost no motivational force until they are close. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md).
- **Temptation bundling** [research] — Milkman's technique of locking a pleasure to a should-task (audiobooks only at the gym; raised attendance 29–51%). A direct patch for steep delay discounting; untested in ADHD specifically. More: [evidence-based-strategies](strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md).
- **Time blindness** [clinical] — Descriptive clinical shorthand — not a DSM/ICD diagnosis — for ADHD's measurable timing problems: perceiving, estimating, and feeling the passage and approach of time. Design consequence: never assume the user can feel duration; the app holds time visibly. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md).
- **Time blocking** [design] — Assigning tasks to calendar slots in advance. Manual versions shatter on the first slip ("collapsed by 10am on Monday") and the wreckage becomes calendar anxiety; what auto-schedulers prove ADHDers actually love is guilt-free automatic *re*-scheduling. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **Timeboxing** [design] — Giving work a fixed time container ("45 minutes on taxes, then stop") rather than working to completion. In the corpus it appears as sprint timeboxes that create the near deadlines ADHD responds to, and as Sunsama-style daily rituals — with the caveat that estimate-dependence collides with time blindness. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md), [app-landscape](product/app-landscape.md).
- **Trusted system** [design] — GTD's requirement that the external system reliably re-presents what it captures; the mind releases an open loop only when it trusts the system to bring it back, and one silently dropped item can collapse that trust. The root of Klyr's never-silently-lose-anything contract. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md), [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).

## V

- **Variable-ratio reinforcement** [research] — Reward delivered on an unpredictable schedule: the most powerful schedule in behavioral psychology and the mechanism of slot machines, loot boxes, and infinite feeds. Klyr's red line: vary delight freely, never vary *whether* the user's work is acknowledged. More: [dopamine-and-motivation](foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md), [motivation-and-gamification](strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md).

## W

- **Waiting mode** [community] — The day-consuming suspension before a scheduled event: nothing can be started because "I'd just have to stop." Widely reported, essentially unstudied — strong UX truth, weak science — and treated by the corpus as a first-class day-shape problem. More: [time-perception](foundations/time-perception.md).
- **Wall of Awful** [community] — Brendan Mahan's model of the emotional barrier built from accumulated failures around a task, which explains why a "simple" task can be unstartable. What helps is climbing it or installing a door — never staring at it, going around, or Hulk-smashing through. More: [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md), [task-initiation-and-paralysis](daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md).
- **Weekly review** [design] — GTD's recurring ritual of emptying inboxes and re-deciding priorities. For ADHD it is the canonical single point of failure — boring, delayed-reward, EF-heavy — so Klyr automates the review instead of assigning it. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **WIP limit** [design] — Kanban's cap on simultaneous work-in-progress. Externally enforces finishing over starting — a structural counter to ADHD's start-everything gravity. More: [planning-methodologies-and-adhd](strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md).
- **Working memory** [research] — The mental scratchpad that holds and manipulates information over seconds. One of the most consistently impaired functions in ADHD (moderate group deficits, far from universal), producing lost threads, evaporated intentions, and forgotten step two — a failure of in-the-moment performance, not knowledge. More: [memory-and-object-permanence](foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md), [executive-function](foundations/executive-function.md).
