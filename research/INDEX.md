# Corpus Index

This corpus is the evidence base for every design decision in Klyr — a life organizer, task manager, and project manager built from the ground up for people with ADHD. This index is the fastest route in: use **Start here** if you are new, the **annotated tree** to see scope, the **routing table** when you have a task, and the **tag index** when you have a topic. Every research doc carries a TL;DR and a design-implications section, so you can usually route, skim, and decide without a full read.

## Start here

Read in this order if you are new to the corpus:

1. [00-executive-summary.md](00-executive-summary.md) — the whole corpus in about 10 minutes.
2. [product/design-principles.md](product/design-principles.md) — the binding constitution: 20 testable principles. Violations are defects, not debates.
3. [product/anti-patterns.md](product/anti-patterns.md) — the never-do-this catalog: 24 patterns with market evidence and alternatives.
4. Keep [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md) open for vocabulary — every term is tagged by status (clinical, research, community, contested, design).
