# Klyr Research Corpus

## TL;DR

- This directory is the **evidence base for every design decision in Klyr** — a life organizer, task manager, and project manager built from the ground up for people with ADHD.
- It contains 29 documents: 20 sourced research docs (foundations, daily life, strategies, product), 4 synthesis docs, navigation files, and 2 meta docs — 553 cited sources in total.
- **Navigate via [INDEX.md](INDEX.md)**: start-here path, annotated tree, a task-to-docs routing table, and a tag index. In a hurry, read [00-executive-summary.md](00-executive-summary.md).
- The synthesis layer is binding: [design-principles.md](product/design-principles.md) and [anti-patterns.md](product/anti-patterns.md) treat violations as defects, not debates.
- How it was built, and how much to trust each kind of claim: [_meta/research-process.md](_meta/research-process.md).
- To add or edit a doc, follow the contract in [_meta/CONVENTIONS.md](_meta/CONVENTIONS.md) — format, sourcing hierarchy, status labels for community terms, and tone.

## What this is

The design-research foundation for Klyr. The product goal it serves: feel intuitive, stay easy to maintain, and help users actually do what they need and want to do — without pressure or shame. Docs are written for Klyr's builders (humans and AI agents): rigorous, specific, and actionable, with a `## Design implications for Klyr` section in every research doc. It is not medical advice and not end-user content.

## How it was built

By a multi-agent research pipeline in July 2026: a 15-document research sweep, a gap analysis that commissioned 5 more docs, a synthesis phase (principles, feature directions, anti-patterns, glossary, executive summary, index), and three independent audit passes. Full provenance, methodology, and known limitations — including how to weigh community claims versus peer-reviewed findings — are in [_meta/research-process.md](_meta/research-process.md).

## How to navigate

- **[INDEX.md](INDEX.md)** — always start here. It routes from "I'm working on X" to the two or three docs that should decide X.
- **[00-executive-summary.md](00-executive-summary.md)** — the whole corpus in about 10 minutes.
- **[GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md)** — term lookup; every term carries an evidentiary status tag (clinical, community, contested).
- Every doc opens with a TL;DR, so skimming is a supported reading mode, not a shortcut.

## How to extend

Read [_meta/CONVENTIONS.md](_meta/CONVENTIONS.md) first — it is the contract that keeps the corpus predictable to navigate. In short: correct frontmatter, TL;DR, design implications, tagged sources, no fabricated numbers, status labels on community concepts, non-pathologizing tone. Then register your doc in [INDEX.md](INDEX.md) (tree, routing table, tag index) and add any new terms to [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md).
