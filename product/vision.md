# Klyr Product Vision

*This vision is grounded in the research corpus ([INDEX](../research/INDEX.md)); where this document and the corpus conflict, the corpus wins until the conflict is resolved deliberately.*

P-numbers reference [design-principles](../research/product/design-principles.md), AP-numbers reference [anti-patterns](../research/product/anti-patterns.md), D-numbers reference [feature-directions](../research/product/feature-directions.md) with their confidence grades.

## TL;DR

- Klyr is **the executive function you wear on the outside**: it holds your memory, your time, and your next move, and delivers them where and when the action happens — permanently. A prosthesis, never a training program (P1, P2).
- The science makes this buildable: ADHD is a performance disorder, not a knowledge disorder; planning is the broken step (d = 1.60 in the best adult component study) while execution is largely intact once a concrete, cue-bound plan exists ([memory-and-object-permanence](../research/foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md)).
- Every competitor builds a **place** the user must visit and maintain. Klyr is a **delivery layer** that goes to the user. The pass/fail gate for every design review: *if you ever have to remember to check Klyr, Klyr has failed.*
- The app is not the product; the moments it lands correctly in your day are. The best Klyr session is three seconds on a lock screen; time-in-app is a cost, not a KPI ([outcomes-and-measurement](../research/product/outcomes-and-measurement.md)).
- The market's defining fact is abandonment (median 15-day retention ~3.9%), and the kill point is always the missed day. Klyr expects you to disappear and makes coming back painless: the comeback is the core loop, built like autosave — invisible until needed (P6, [app-landscape](../research/product/app-landscape.md)).
- The first territory is the life load — chores, bills, refills, the dreaded call — where the ADHD tax actually bleeds and no product serves. We win Tuesday night before competing for Monday morning ([daily-life-impact](../research/daily-life/daily-life-impact.md)).
- Six pillars: capture you trust blindly; delivery at the point of performance; time as a physical object; everything leads with a startable sentence; organization is Klyr's chore; warmth is precision, not personality.
- Three loops that close without user memory — capture, daily, comeback. They nest: the daily loop is the comeback loop at small amplitude, and a comeback is just the most expensive start.
- V1 is 15 items skewed to capture, delivery, and time surfaces. The market's warmth features (body doubling, companions, chat) are deliberately deferred — a named risk with a month-three decision gate.
- North Star: **return-after-lapse.** Engagement may veto a change, never justify one; shame-risk and demand-pressure stop conditions are pre-registered; we never A/B test shame (P19).
- Your memory and the basic executive loop are free forever; payment buys depth and scale, never rescue. Zero third-party trackers — everything stored is health data (P18, AP-24).
- The honest risks: the deferred-warmth bet, notification channel death, benefit that works without feeling like it works — and Klyr's own novelty honeymoon, which arrives on schedule like everyone else's.

## 1. Why Klyr exists

An adult with ADHD almost always knows what to do. The gap between knowing and doing *is* the condition: ADHD is a performance disorder, not a knowledge disorder ([executive-function](../research/foundations/executive-function.md)). The three internal resources every productivity tool quietly assumes — self-directed speech, felt time, summoned motivation — are precisely the weak ones. Covert information is "weak as a source of stimulus control"; time collapses into now and not-now (timing deficits across all four lab paradigms, lifespan meta-analytic g ≈ 0.688; [time-perception](../research/foundations/time-perception.md)); delayed payoffs are steeply discounted (d = 0.43) while reward anticipation under-fires (d = 0.48) — *plans don't pull; ticking the box does* ([dopamine-and-motivation](../research/foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md)).

So a to-do list documents intentions — a record of the problem, not a solution. Worse, the standard app is a *pull* system, and "remember to check the app" is itself a prospective-memory task, the exact function that fails ([memory-and-object-permanence](../research/foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md)). Help delivered at planning time mostly does not transfer; it has to land at the point of performance, where control actually breaks ([executive-function](../research/foundations/executive-function.md)).

The cost of going without lands hardest in unpaid, boring, infinitely recurring life work — chores, admin, bills, health upkeep: an ADHD tax of roughly £1,600/year in one UK survey, only ~46% of prescriptions refilled on time in one records study, partners conscripted into the nag role ([daily-life-impact](../research/daily-life/daily-life-impact.md)).

And the tools sold as the fix become the injury. The community post-mortem is one sentence: *"find new system, get excited, customize obsessively, use it perfectly for 10 days, miss one day, feel guilty, avoid it, never open it again"* ([app-landscape](../research/product/app-landscape.md)). The missed day is scheduled — novelty decay and boom-bust capacity are features of ADHD, not user failures — but the abandonment is manufactured: one miss barely dents habit automaticity ([habits-and-routines](../research/daily-life/habits-and-routines.md)). What kills the practice is what the tool displays afterward, landing on a person who arrives pre-shamed and criticism-sensitive (emotional dysregulation in an estimated 34–70% of adults with ADHD; [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](../research/foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md)). *"You feel guilty every time you open it. So you stop opening it."*

What makes this buildable rather than merely sad is an asymmetry: planning is the broken step (d = 1.60) while plan retention and execution are largely intact. Hand someone a decided, cue-bound first step and they finish disproportionately well — if-then plans carry meta-analytic d ≈ 0.65, replicated in ADHD samples ([evidence-based-strategies](../research/strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md)). Externalizing time works the same way: in one RCT, time-assistive devices moved parent-rated daily time management by d = 1.0. You cannot train the sense of time; you can hand someone a clock they can feel.

The missing product is not a better container for tasks. It is a **delivery system for executive control at the moment of action** — trusted blindly, maintained never.

## 2. Who Klyr is for

One primary user: an adult with ADHD — diagnosed or self-recognized; there is no diagnosis gate (P16) — carrying a full life-load: a job, a household's executive burden, admin, people. Over half of diagnosed US adults were diagnosed *as* adults; many arrive carrying relief and grief at once, decades of "not trying hard enough," and the lesson every buried planner taught: organizing tools accuse ([adhd-overview](../research/foundations/adhd-overview.md), [populations-and-variation](../research/foundations/populations-and-variation.md), [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](../research/foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md)).

We design for **states of that one person, not personas** ([populations-and-variation](../research/foundations/populations-and-variation.md)). Across a year the same user is the honeymooner in week one, the steady user in week three, the ghost in week six, and the wary returner in week nine. Medication-window capable at 10 a.m., depleted at 11 p.m. A boom-week overcommitter and a trough-week avoider — capacity collapse is a scheduled event, not an edge case ([when-to-back-off](../research/product/when-to-back-off.md)). And seasonal selves: newly diagnosed; luteal or perimenopausal weeks (user-declared, never inferred); AuDHD seasons where surprise is a threat and change must be announced; older adults who do not live on a widget-covered home screen; the month-three self, for whom nothing here is novel anymore.

Two consequences are law. Defaults are set for the most sensitive state, because capacity is weather, not character (P14). And everything contested — sensory intensity, novelty, pressure, disclosure — ships as a dial with a calm default, never a house style (P16). A prosthesis that works only in your good state is not a prosthesis. The states that decide whether Klyr gets a year two are the ghost and the returner; everyone passes through them.

## 3. The bet

Klyr's position, straight from the corpus: **the tool that expects you to disappear and makes coming back painless** ([app-landscape](../research/product/app-landscape.md)). Our way of getting there is the disagreement with the market. Every competitor builds a place the user goes to manage tasks — and each solves one mechanism, so users run 3–5-app stacks; ADHD-native apps are day planners without depth; power tools are shame machines; nobody owns the restart or the point of performance. All share one assumption: the user will come to the app and maintain it. That assumption is exactly what ADHD makes expensive. We believe five things competitors do not:

1. **The app is not the product; the moments it lands correctly in your day are.** Klyr is push-first: cues go to the user, at the moment of action, with the decided first step attached. The best session is three seconds on a lock screen. Time-in-app is a cost ([outcomes-and-measurement](../research/product/outcomes-and-measurement.md)). The binding gate for every review: *if you ever have to remember to check Klyr, Klyr has failed* (P1, P2).
2. **The lapse cannot be prevented, only survived.** The industry tries to out-notify neurobiology with streaks and engagement pings; that poisons the return. We spend nothing preventing absence and everything on the reunion — the first *return*, not the first session, decides the relationship, and no product designs for it ([app-landscape](../research/product/app-landscape.md), P6).
3. **The serial tool-switcher is the market, not churn.** A ~3.9% day-15 retention baseline means nearly the entire market re-enters play every few months. Being easy to leave — free export, cheap cancellation, imports that respect where you came from — is why people stay, and why they come back (D44 promising).
4. **The first territory is the life load, not the work calendar.** Impairment scales with how boring, recurring, multi-step, and audience-free a task is; the tax bleeds at rent, refills, laundry, and the dreaded 20-minute form — a surface no product treats as first-class ([daily-life-impact](../research/daily-life/daily-life-impact.md)). We win Tuesday night before competing for Monday morning.
5. **Blind trust is the moat.** Capture that never loses a word, a reminder channel that never lies and never spams, an archive that answers "everything I ever captured." Features get copied; a trust record compounds. Competitors bet on richer in-app experiences and AI theater — we bet that for this user, the winning interface is mostly *not being an interface*.

## 4. Pillars

**1. Capture you trust blindly.** A thought is captured in under three seconds, zero decisions, from anywhere, before any account exists — then never silently lost, altered, or expired; searchable forever; exportable in one tap. The mind releases an open loop only when it trusts retrieval, and one dropped item collapses the prosthesis ([memory-and-object-permanence](../research/foundations/memory-and-object-permanence.md); P3, P4). **Rules out:** required fields at capture, account walls before first value, expiry, sync that can eat text (AP-14, AP-20, AP-21).

**2. Deliver at the point of performance.** Klyr is push-first: cues arrive where and when the action happens, each carrying the decided first move in the notification body; event anchors beat clock times (if-then binding, d ≈ 0.65; [evidence-based-strategies](../research/strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md)); glanceable surfaces outrank in-app dashboards; and notification trust is a budgeted currency — few, actionable, batched, with persistence reserved for a tiny set of user-designated critical anchors (P1, P2, P15). **Rules out:** features whose value depends on opening the app or holding a planning ritual (AP-16), engagement pings of any kind (AP-11), and any notification that names a task without an action — "Report" is a defect.

**3. Time is a physical object Klyr holds.** Duration renders as space everywhere it matters; reminder salience follows a J-curve into the real deadline window; departures get leaving-soon countdowns to *stop-doing-things* time; transitions are buffered; schedules self-heal legibly; defaults respect delayed chronotypes ([time-perception](../research/foundations/time-perception.md); P9; AP-19). The sharpest case is the life deadline: bills, refills, renewals, and appointments are lead-time deadlines with a runway Klyr holds, surfaced early enough to act and framed as tax avoided ("renewal caught 5 days early") — never a tally of what forgetting cost ([daily-life-impact](../research/daily-life/daily-life-impact.md); D10 promising). **Rules out:** numerals-only deadlines, uniform daily nags, fake countdowns, and ambient red urgency — urgency is user-summoned equipment or it does not exist (AP-8, AP-9).

**4. Everything leads with a startable sentence.** Every surface — list, widget, reminder — leads with a concrete next physical action, not a task noun, and Klyr does the decomposing, because planning is the impaired step and execution is not ([task-initiation-and-paralysis](../research/daily-life/task-initiation-and-paralysis.md); P8). Any task is one tap from its 2-minute version; a one-thing-now mode and an "I'm stuck" collapse are always one tap away (P12). AI serves this invisibly — suggest, preview, undo; never a silent write, never a chat front door ([ai-assistance-for-adhd](../research/product/ai-assistance-for-adhd.md); P17). **Rules out:** lists of unstartable nouns, "just break it down" homework, blank canvases that make the user invent the system (AP-15), and grading days as perfect-or-failed (AP-7).

**5. Organization is Klyr's chore, not yours.** No weekly review, no filing, no grooming — ever. Klyr reviews continuously (one keep/shrink/someday/let-go tap at natural moments), ages items into amnesty, compresses missed cycles into one next instance, reflows plans with visible reasoning, auto-closes days blamelessly, and stays correct after two weeks of silence — every automatic move legible and reversible ([planning-methodologies-and-adhd](../research/strategies/planning-methodologies-and-adhd.md); P5, P6, P7, P17). This machinery is autosave: invisible until needed, never discussed on the daily surface — no "when you fall off" copy anywhere in the product. **Rules out:** ritual dependency, maintenance debt, overdue arithmetic — a 47-item red pile is structurally impossible, not suppressed (AP-1, AP-16, AP-19, AP-21).

**6. Warmth is precision, not personality.** The known risk of this frame is coldness, so warmth is placed deliberately, not sprayed. It lives in exactly three moments: instant, unconditional acknowledgment of every action — starts and partials included, delight may vary but arrival never does (P10, P11; [dopamine-and-motivation](../research/foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md)); the comeback, the most-designed flow in the product (P6); and copy that passes "would a good ADHD coach say this mid-shame-spiral?" (P5; [emotional-regulation-and-rsd](../research/foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md)). Motivation invites and never coerces: pressure is opt-in equipment with one-tap renegotiation, repeated deferrals *lower* the pressure (a demand-sensitivity defense; [motivation-and-gamification](../research/strategies/motivation-and-gamification.md)), rewards aim only at aversive tasks and never at what the user already loves, and a one-tap no-explanation low-day dial makes the user the sensor (P14). Everywhere else, warmth *is* calm utility: to someone whose tools have been disappointed parents, the app that never accuses, never lies about time, and never loses a word is what care feels like. A good prosthesis loves you by working. **Rules out:** punishment mechanics, streak hostage-taking, guilt copy, leaderboards, casino mechanics, mental-state inference, mascots and chatty personas in v1 (AP-2 through AP-6, AP-10, AP-22).

## 5. The core loops

Three loops, each closing without user memory, each surviving being skipped. They nest — **the daily loop is the comeback loop at small amplitude**, and **a comeback is just the most expensive start** — so one engine serves the resume after lunch, the derailed Tuesday, and the three-week silence (P6, P7).

**Capture loop (seconds → days).** Stray thought → captured in under three seconds (lock screen, widget, share sheet, voice, overlay) → visible "got it," back to exactly what you were doing → the mind lets go. Later, at a natural moment, Klyr asks *one* skippable question ("next step, a date, or leave it parked?") → the item becomes a startable sentence bound to a cue → it sleeps → it lands at its point of performance. The loop closes when the cue fires, never when the user remembers; parked items provably come back, which is what makes the next capture happen.

**Daily loop (one day).** The day assembles itself: calendar skeleton, 1–3 protected items, bench beneath, warm-up-first ordering by declared state — never hardest-first as doctrine, which suits only 7% of surveyed ADHDers ([evidence-based-strategies](../research/strategies/evidence-based-strategies.md)) → cues land where the user is, each carrying the decided first move → the user acts; acknowledgment is instant and unconditional; starts and partials count → each exit offers a skippable one-line resume note; the next pre-chosen action surfaces with no re-decision gap → stuck collapses everything to one small action → when reality diverges, the plan reflows with one line of reasoning and undo; wreckage never renders → the day auto-closes blamelessly. The user's minimum obligation to this loop is zero — every step survives being skipped.

**Comeback loop (days → months).** Silence → no guilt pings, ever → Klyr quietly tidies: amnesty parks aging items with traces, cycles compress to one next instance, plans reflow; on a declared low day, recurring items skip rather than stack and only user-designated critical anchors keep their persistence → the return lands on a calm, current screen: neutral hello, honest "here's what we tidied — undo anything," one small next action plus the last resume note → the restart is acknowledged as the win it is → full state, zero make-up work, no gap arithmetic. Eventually another silence — expected, unfeared.

## 6. What Klyr is not

- **Not a medical device or treatment.** Klyr never diagnoses, screens, detects, or treats, and no copy implies otherwise (P20; [when-to-back-off](../research/product/when-to-back-off.md)).
- **Not a training program.** No graduation mechanics, no fading reminders as a "reward," no brain-training minigames — gains persist only as long as the scaffold does (P1; AP-23).
- **Not an AI companion.** Task-anchored assistance only; no open-ended chat relationship, no sycophancy — the corpus draws a hard no-companion line ([ai-assistance-for-adhd](../research/product/ai-assistance-for-adhd.md)).
- **Not a Notion-style canvas.** Klyr ships working defaults; users never build the system to get one (AP-15).
- **Not a social network.** No feeds, no leaderboards, no public failure; any future presence features are chosen-peer and opt-in (AP-6).
- **Not a surveillance wellness app.** No mental-state inference, no mood mining, no third-party trackers — everything stored is health data (P18; AP-22, AP-24).
- **Not a gamified casino.** No paid or scarcity-mediated randomness; acknowledgment is never a slot machine (AP-10).
- **Not an engagement business.** DAU is not a goal; a shorter, better-placed session always beats a longer one (P19).

## 7. V1 scope

The smallest Klyr that embodies all six pillars — deliberately skewed toward capture, delivery, and time surfaces, because that is the prosthesis's spine. D-numbers and confidence grades from [feature-directions](../research/product/feature-directions.md).

1. **Instant capture everywhere + the nothing-lost trust surface** (D1 evidence-backed; D2, D44 promising): widget, lock screen, share sheet, voice, two-keystroke desktop overlay; sub-3-second, zero fields, first capture before any account; one search over everything ever captured; undo-not-confirm; one-tap export free forever; Todoist/Notion/plain-text import that lands in drip triage.
2. **Next-action extraction + task shrinking** (D3, D12 evidence-backed; D39 promising): every item one tap from a startable sentence and a 2-minute version; granularity dial; suggest → preview → undo only.
3. **If-then cue binding** (D5 evidence-backed): event anchors as the first-class reminder type; bare clock times offered conversion ("5 p.m. — is that 'when you close your laptop'?").
4. **Drip-fed clarification** (D4 promising): one skippable question at natural moments; no inbox-zero surface exists anywhere.
5. **Glanceable ambient surface** (D42 promising, D27 evidence-backed): widget and lock screen with 1–3 rotating next actions plus time-left-in-block. This is the primary product surface, not an accessory.
6. **Reminder engine with a trust budget + low-day dial** (D37 promising; D36-lite promising): two classes only — point-of-performance interrupts and batched digests — action always in the body, "why this fired" visible, quiet hours default-on; snooze-death breaker at ~3 snoozes; three ignores change strategy, never volume; one-tap renegotiation with zero penalty; a no-explanation low-day dial that quiets everything but user-designated critical anchors and shrinks today.
7. **Visual time: J-curve salience + leaving-soon mode** (D27 evidence-backed; D29 promising): shapes beside numerals everywhere durations matter; salience concentrated in the final window; stop-doing-things countdowns before departures.
8. **Life deadlines with runway** (D10 promising, scoped to the deadline species): bills, refills, renewals, and appointments as first-class objects with lead-time defaults and concretized first steps — the ADHD tax attacked at the point of performance; no budgeting module, no health suite.
9. **Tiny day plan on the calendar skeleton** (D6, D43, D13 promising): 1–3 protected items plus bench; warm-up-first state-based ordering with frog-first only ever offered; *read-only* calendar sync in v1 — two-way waits, because sync bugs strike the P4 trust contract.
10. **Self-healing reflow, legible** (D7 promising): automatic replan with one-line reasoning and undo; no rescheduled-count ever shown.
11. **Amnesty, resurfacing, elastic recurrence** (D23, D24 promising): aging items de-emphasize and park with visible traces — overdue cannot render; missed cycles compress into one next instance; recurring items skip, not stack, on declared low days; parked items return a few at a time, changing form: "Still matter? Shrink it? Let it go?"
12. **Automated review + blameless auto-close** (D22 promising): days and weeks close themselves; recaps lead with what happened — "what moved, what carried," never "what you missed"; repeated carries get the intentionality question.
13. **The restart ritual** (D26 promising): the hero flow — neutral welcome, tidied state with an undoable trace, one small re-entry action; restart latency instrumented from the first build.
14. **Instant acknowledgment + finish states beyond done + resume bookmarks** (D30, D19 evidence-backed; D21 promising): sub-400 ms, unconditional; started / partial / paused are first-class celebrated states; every exit offers a skippable one-line breadcrumb, rendered first on reopen.
15. **One-thing-now, the I'm-stuck collapse, calm intensity presets** (D15, D17, D38 promising): the whole UI collapses to one small action on demand; motion, sound, and celebration dials default calm; literal copy as house style.

Direction 28 (learned duration correction, promising) runs dark in v1: actuals are collected, nothing is surfaced until confidence is real — a wrong correction spends trust.

**Deliberately not in v1:**

- **The warmth and presence cluster** — body doubling (D16), companion creature (D35), bounded unstick chat (D41), all promising: the market's most-loved and highest-willingness-to-pay features, deferred so the calm substrate can prove itself solo and social RSD surfaces stay at zero. This is the vision's riskiest cut; it gets a pre-committed month-three decision gate (see risks).
- **The urgency equipment rack** (D34 promising) and temptation bundling / dopamine menus (D33 promising): the house stays calm until pressure and reward layering can be genuinely opt-in equipment with tripwires.
- **Novelty rotation content** (D31 promising): the surface/substrate split is architectural from day one; the wardrobe ships when month-three data says boredom, not friction, is the churn driver.
- **Energy windows and cycle-aware options** (D9 promising): sensitive-tier features must earn their consent architecture first (P18).
- **Full Essentials mode** (D36 promising): v1's low-day dial plus skip-not-stack plus amnesty carries troughs; the deeper anchors-only mode with a passive support surface follows it.
- **Waiting-mode layout (D8), routine menus (D11), soft landings (D20)** — all promising; leaving-soon covers the departure half of D8 in v1.
- **Dread receipts** (D25 speculative): dark-launch data collection only, per its own risk note.
- **Deep project hierarchies, two-way calendar sync (D43), shared household lists (D45), any opt-in streak mode** (AP-3 package — possibly never). A v1 "project" is a parked bundle with a next action; long-horizon depth is the year-two move into the market's third gap ([app-landscape](../research/product/app-landscape.md)).

Several deferrals are the market's most-loved patterns. Cutting them is a deliberate cost, priced in section 12.

## 8. How it should feel

Like a good prosthesis: you notice that it works, not that it is there. Five qualities, all testable:

- **Instant.** Interaction feedback lands under ~400 ms; cold starts and sync spinners are motivational taxes, not polish issues ([ux-design-for-adhd](../research/product/ux-design-for-adhd.md); P11).
- **Calm.** One visually primary action per screen; salience is a rationed budget of one spend per screen; red is never the resting color of anything (P12, P14). Density and celebration intensity are the user's dials, defaulting low — stimulation-seekers turn it up; nobody has to turn it down (P16).
- **Literal.** Buttons say what they do. No idioms, sarcasm, or action-hiding cuteness on functional controls; dyslexia-safe typography as the floor ([populations-and-variation](../research/foundations/populations-and-variation.md), [ux-design-for-adhd](../research/product/ux-design-for-adhd.md)).
- **Legible.** Every automatic move shows one line of reasoning and offers undo; "hidden, not lost" is stated in the UI (P17, P4).
- **Quietly kind.** Every sentence passes the coach-mid-shame-spiral test; error states blame the system; self-compassion is a designable lever, used sparingly and never as lecture ([emotional-regulation-and-rsd](../research/foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md); AP-5). No exclamation-point cheerleading as house style — the voice is a competent, unbothered assistant who has seen your mess and is not worried.

The feeling that matters most is the one on reopening after an absence: not dread, not a wall — relief. If the honest description of any surface is "a disappointed parent," it does not ship.

## 9. Success

**North Star: return-after-lapse** ([outcomes-and-measurement](../research/product/outcomes-and-measurement.md); P19). Of users who go quiet after regular use, the share who come back within the window and do something meaningful in their first session — where completing, shrinking, or consciously releasing an item all count, mirroring amnesty's own choices — without a guilt prompt having fired. Idle and return thresholds are calibration questions, not commitments; verdicts are read at month three or later, never on honeymoon data (P13). Beneath it, hypothesis-grade behavioral proxies: **restart latency** (reopen → first meaningful action), **resurfaced-item action rate**, and **capture-to-next-action** — did the thing we held come back at the right moment and turn into action? Functional tier: opt-in validated instruments (WFIRS-S or AAQoL), quarterly at most, aggregate only, never gates.

**Guardrails — watched, never optimized; stop conditions pre-registered:**
1. **Shame risk:** overdue-anxiety growth, lapse-then-never-return churn attributable to specific surfaces, negative check-in sentiment. We never A/B test shame or pressure; there is no equipoise.
2. **Amnesty-undo rate:** if users keep reversing auto-tidies, the machine is eating things they still wanted — "the app deleted my task" is a P4 violation in the user's eyes.
3. **Notification trust:** action rate per notification and ignore/override trends; habituation is channel death, and the channel is the product. Three ignores trigger a strategy change, never more volume.
4. **Demand pressure:** deferral rates rising after prompts or offers auto-de-escalates the feature — the demand-sensitivity tripwire.
5. **Real-world safety:** user-designated critical anchors (rent-class, refill-class) must not be missed more during low-day or quiet weeks than baseline. Forgiveness that quietly costs someone their rent is a failed design, not a calm one.
6. **Time-in-app, watched as a cost:** for an overwhelm-reducing tool, more is often worse.

**Anti-metrics — banned as success measures:** DAU, streak length, task throughput, time-in-app, overdue counts, honeymoon NPS (P19). And a standing Goodhart clause: any activation count — starts, completions, captures per user — is a diagnostic, never a target. The moment we maximize it, we have built the nag.

## 10. Trust economics

One subscription at a transparent, stable price inside the documented $30–100/year ADHD-native norm, sold honestly as cheaper than the 3–5-app stack it replaces ([app-landscape](../research/product/app-landscape.md)). The split is principled, and it protects the thesis: **your memory and the basic executive loop are free forever.** Capture, search, archive, export, the tiny day plan, basic point-of-performance reminders, the widget, amnesty, and the restart never cost money and never lapse — a free user still gets a push-first prosthesis, not a documented-intentions app. Payment buys depth and scale: unlimited AI breakdown and shrinking, self-healing schedules, the expanded life-deadline runway, advanced time surfaces, and household features later. Payment buys *more Klyr*, never relief from manufactured pain and never the difference between being helped and being accused.

The rails, all binding: payment state never touches data — read and export stay free forever (AP-13); trials warn loudly and never convert silently; cancellation is in-app in two steps or fewer (AP-12); no dynamic pricing; no paid randomness (AP-10); a long-inactive subscriber is offered a pause instead of a silent renewal — we will not earn a dollar from prospective-memory failure; and resubscribing lands on the restart ritual, not a guilt screen.

Privacy is architecture, not copy (P18; [privacy-and-data-ethics](../research/product/privacy-and-data-ethics.md)): zero third-party ad or analytics SDKs anywhere (AP-24); two-tier storage from day one — recoverable encryption for the task graph, local-first and optionally E2E for sensitive fields; behavioral exhaust ages out by default; "forget this period" is honest, propagating deletion; consent is just-in-time, per-category, default-off. Claims stay inside the evidence: a tool, never a treatment; "many people find…" for community strategies, "research shows…" only where it does (P20; AP-23). In this market, billing and privacy honesty is not overhead — it is the positioning.

## 11. Platform posture

Klyr lives where the moments are, not where the dashboard is. That means, in priority order: the phone's lock screen, notification shade, and widgets; the share sheet and voice input; a two-keystroke overlay on the desktop where work happens; and only then the app itself — glanceable surfaces outrank in-app screens in roadmap priority (P2). Capture must work offline and never wait on a network round-trip: feedback is local and sub-second, AI parsing runs after, off the critical path (P3; [ai-assistance-for-adhd](../research/product/ai-assistance-for-adhd.md)).

The calendar is read as the day's skeleton in v1 and written to not at all; two-way sync arrives only when it cannot betray the trust contract (D43; P4). And because the delivery channel is OS-controlled real estate, redundancy is the hedge: if notifications are throttled, the widget still shows the next action; if both fail, the app still opens on a calm, current today. Push is the spine, but every surface degrades into a simpler one that still works. No technology-stack commitments belong in this document — but any stack that cannot deliver sub-second capture feedback and reliably fresh ambient surfaces is disqualified by the vision itself.

## 12. Risks and open questions

1. **The warmth bet may be wrong.** Relatedness is the most monetized need in the category — coaching runs $99–345/month, and body doubling is the community's most-loved starter (85% of 220 surveyed neurodivergent adults; community-validated, no controlled trials) — and v1 ships none of it. Mitigation is a pre-committed gate: at month three, lapse-return and churn data decide whether the presence cluster (D16, D35, D41) moves up. If "calm utility is the warmth" fails, we deferred exactly the features that would have held people.
2. **Channel death.** The thesis rides on notifications and widgets — OS-throttled real estate where habituation is documented (identical alerts overridden at 49–96% rates in clinical settings). The J-curve, form-shifting resurfacing, the trust budget, and multi-surface redundancy are mitigations built on parameters the corpus openly flags as untested.
3. **Objective benefit without felt benefit.** In the time-externalization RCT, observer-rated function moved (d = 1.0) while self-ratings did not. A prosthesis can work without feeling like it works; retention and word of mouth run on feelings, and P20 forbids closing that gap with claims. We need felt-benefit surfaces (blameless recaps, competence evidence) and honest instrumentation of whether they land.
4. **Anchor and amnesty fragility.** Event cues are the best-evidenced delivery channel, but anchors are unreliable in chaotic lives, and every wrong-moment cue spends the trust budget a correct one earns. Amnesty is a knife-edge: too fast reads as deletion, too slow lets piles form; every threshold is a placeholder for real-user calibration ([anti-patterns](../research/product/anti-patterns.md), open questions).
5. **Ordinary bugs are trust breaches.** V1 is fifteen mostly ambient, OS-integrated systems; a stale widget or one eaten capture is not a defect ticket but a P4 breach of the only contract that matters. Engineering quality is product strategy, and this is the widest gap between ambition and reality in the plan.
6. **The frame underweights emotional paralysis.** Procrastination is mood repair, and the right sentence at the right moment can still lose to the Wall of Awful ([emotional-regulation-and-rsd](../research/foundations/emotional-regulation-and-rsd.md)). V1's answers — shrinking, the stuck collapse, amnesty, pressure valves — are structural, not emotional. Some users will need the mood-side equipment sooner than we plan to ship it; the month-three gate exists for them too.
7. **Forgiveness may dissolve stakes, and the life wedge may be refused.** Whether forgiving metrics sustain behavior as well as stakes is untested ([habits-and-routines](../research/daily-life/habits-and-routines.md)); over-elastic recurrence can stretch consequences until plants die — the anchor guardrail exists for exactly this. And users may not want bills and refills inside a task app ([daily-life-impact](../research/daily-life/daily-life-impact.md) flags it open); if the life wedge is rejected, a simpler day planner beats us on calm.
8. **Klyr's own honeymoon.** The novelty honeymoon is named in the corpus as Klyr's single biggest product risk ([dopamine-and-motivation](../research/foundations/dopamine-and-motivation.md)) — and it applies to Klyr. Every user's excitement will fade on schedule; v1 defers novelty rotation and bets that quiet correctness survives boredom better than fresh paint. If month-three data disagrees, rotation moves up (P13). Related: quiet utility demos badly in a market performing AI theater, the free layer may suppress conversion, and the North Star deliberately spends money engagement dashboards would bank. This vision only works if the company holds P19 under growth pressure — that is a governance risk, and naming it here is the first control.

**Open questions we are obligated to test, not settle by taste:** amnesty pacing; urgency dosage and decay; the right default density and celebration intensity; restart-ritual tone (celebration vs. condescension); whether return-after-lapse predicts LTV; rotation cadence; how to measure felt benefit without manufacturing self-judgment. Until a question is answered, the calm, forgiving, autonomy-preserving option is the default ([design-principles](../research/product/design-principles.md)).
