# product/ — product definition

What we have **decided to build**, as distinct from [`research/`](../research/INDEX.md), which is what we **know**. Docs here are bound by the corpus: every load-bearing claim cites it, and where a product doc and the corpus conflict, the corpus wins until the conflict is resolved deliberately (and the resolution written down).

| Doc | What it is |
|---|---|
| [vision.md](vision.md) | The product vision: the bet, six pillars, three core loops, v1 scope (15 items + spine + cut order), North Star and guardrails, trust economics. Synthesized from four judged candidate visions; verified against the binding principles and anti-patterns. |

Note the namespace difference: `research/product/` holds *evidence synthesis* (design principles, anti-patterns, feature directions — graded against sources); `product/` holds *decisions* (what v1 is, what we defer, what we refuse).

Conventions: light frontmatter (`title`, `file`, `status: living|draft|frozen`, `updated`, `summary`, `tags`), P/AP/D-number references resolve against the corpus synthesis docs, corpus links are relative (`../research/...`).
