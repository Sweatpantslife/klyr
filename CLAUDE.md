# Klyr

Klyr is a life-organizer / task-manager / project-manager designed from the ground up for people with ADHD: intuitive, easy to maintain, and built to help users actually do what they need and want to do — without pressure or shame.

## Repo state

Research phase. The design-research corpus lives in `research/`.

- Start at `research/INDEX.md` (once present) for navigation.
- Corpus format and rules: `research/_meta/CONVENTIONS.md`.
- Provenance and limitations: `research/_meta/research-process.md`.
